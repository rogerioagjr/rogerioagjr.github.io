I was a Teaching Assistant (2023) and later Head Teaching Assistant (2024) for
[EE 148b: Large Language and Vision
Models](https://gkioxari.github.io/teaching/cs148/) at Caltech. This course
introduces the foundations, design, training, and evaluation of large language
models and vision-language models, with an emphasis on modern multimodal
systems, reasoning, alignment, and emerging research directions.
